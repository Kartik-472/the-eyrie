Bite-Sized Financial Masterclass

Learn at your own pace with 5 to 15 minute quick courses designed by financial advisors.

⏱️ 5-Min Quick Course: The Emergency Fund Rule

Duration: 5 mins | Beginner Level

An emergency fund is your financial safety net. Aim to stash away 3 to 6 months of essential living expenses in a high-yield savings account so unexpected job loss or medical bills never force you into high-interest debt.

📖 15-Min Masterclass: Introduction to Index Funds & ETFs

Duration: 15 mins | Intermediate Level

Instead of guessing which single stock will skyrocket, index funds let you buy a tiny slice of hundreds of top companies (like Apple, Microsoft, Amazon) all at once. This automatic diversification drastically lowers risk for long-term investors.

📖 10-Min Masterclass: Understanding Credit Scores & Interest

Duration: 10 mins | Beginner Level

Your credit score determines how banks view your reliability. Paying credit card bills in full every month keeps your utilization low and unlocks lower mortgage and loan interest rates over your lifetime.
